## Breviter

- **Professor associatus**, Departamentum Litterarum, Universitas de los Andes (ab anno 2019); eiusdem Departamenti prefectus.
- Professor assistens, Departamentum Philosophie, Universitas de los Andes (2016–2018).
- Professor associatus, Departamentum Humanitatum, Universitas Jorge Tadeo Lozano (2013–2016).
- **Doctor philosophie (DPhil)**, Collegium Balliolense, Universitas Oxoniensis (2014), dissertatione de semantica Guillelmi de Ockham conscripta.
- Baccalaureus (2004) et studia superiora (2009) in philosophia, Universitas Nationalis Columbie.
- Socius Societatis Internationalis pro Studio Philosophie Medii Evi (SIEPM).
